§ 03.A — About us

Who we are

AI Skunkworks is one of the most active graduate student units at Northeastern for research and builds in modern AI. We give students a platform to ship real work — not just coursework — in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and deep learning. Founded 2019, quiet through 2022–2024, revived January 2026 — the mission hasn't moved.

Formed under the guidance of Professor Nik Bear Brown (Computer Science Ph.D., UCLA). He remains faculty advisor, bringing 10+ years of AI/ML industry experience to guide researchers. Students learn the stack, then lead the next cohort — a knowledge-transfer loop. Core principle: learn by doing.

We prefer real problems over theory theater. Active collaborations include Harvard Medical School and Boston Children's Hospital on computational biology research. In-house work spans visual analytics, causal inference, model interpretability, and the rest of the active divisions on the projects board.

§ 03.B — Year archives

Cohort timeline

2019 — founding cohort ▾

AI Skunkworks launched at Northeastern as a student-led collective for experimental ML work outside the formal curriculum.

2020 — early expansion ▾

Second cohort grows the research groups and early project files. Remote-year adaptations begin as campus operations shift.

2021 — project density ▾

Peak pre-quiet activity: Explainable AI deployments, competition work, and mentorship pairings across Khoury and allied labs.

2022–2024 — quiet period ▾

Operations paused while founders graduated and projects went dormant. The name and mission persisted informally.

2025 — recon phase ▾

Informal reconvening among alumni and faculty ahead of the formal 2026 revival. Charter drafts and division outlines circulate privately.

2026 — revival cohort ▾

Officially revived January 6, 2026. New command structure, new projects, roster filling in as crews spin up.

§ 03.C — Operating principle

How we operate

PRINCIPLE · 01

Open by default

Research, code, and failures get published, not just wins. The postmortem is part of the deliverable.

PRINCIPLE · 02

Small teams, real stakes

No busywork. Every project ships or dies visibly — short loops, honest status, no slide-deck theater.

PRINCIPLE · 03

Mentorship over hierarchy

Faculty and industry mentors advise, they don't gatekeep. If you carry a project, you're crew.
